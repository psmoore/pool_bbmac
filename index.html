Brian Bent Memorial Aquatics Complex

Coronado Unified's aquatics complex beside Coronado High School: an Olympic-size competition pool set up long course or short course, and a warm six-lane instructional pool. Lap swim, Masters, youth swim and water polo clubs, swim lessons, Coronado High teams and visiting teams all share the water. Every time on these pages comes from the pools' live schedule, so when BBMAC changes it, these pages change with it.

What this replaces

  • A PDF calendar every month, revised mid-month. Lap swim lives in monthly PDFs ("October 2026 - Revised"), so a change means re-making and re-posting the file. Here the staff change the schedule once and every page follows.
  • "*Revised - Comp Pool - lap swim open 10am-2pm." The Lap Swim page carries a revision note without saying which day it means. Here the change is on the day it applies, in the pool it applies to.
  • "Hours can fluctuate based on rental needs and events on a day by day basis." Rentals, meets and tournaments aren't on the calendar, so swimmers can't tell which lanes are left. Here every booking holds its lanes, and lap swim shows what is really open.
  • Long course or short course? The calendar says LC or SC in each day's box. Here the lane-by-lane view shows the pool the way it is set up that day.
  • Masters and clubs on their own websites. Coronado Masters, Different Strokes, five youth water polo clubs and Sea to Desert each post their own times, and BBMAC's pages send you off to find them. Here they can be one schedule.
  • Visiting teams booking blind. Teams request water through Facilitron without seeing what's already booked. Here the lane-by-lane calendar shows the open water first.
