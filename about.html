About the Aquatics Complex

The BBMAC facility is versatile, allowing us to accommodate the specific needs of our patrons.

Competition pool

  • Olympic size
  • Long course: eight 50-meter lanes
  • Short course: 20 × 25-yard lanes
  • Two full-size water polo courses
  • 7 ft 0 in deep throughout
  • 79–80°F

Instructional pool

  • 25 yards, six lanes
  • 3 ft 6 in to 5 ft 6 in deep
  • 86–89°F, for children's swim lessons and water therapy
  • Handicap lift available

Each combined restroom/locker room has two showers and lockers for day use; locks are not provided. A family restroom and several outdoor heated showers are available.

Cross training

BBMAC has direct access to a weight room and to Coronado High School's track-and-field facilities. Coronado also offers ocean and bay swimming, and bike, kayak and stand-up paddleboard rentals.
