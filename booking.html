Booking the Pool

Whether your team or organization needs just a few lanes or the entire pool, BBMAC is happy to work with you. BBMAC has hosted water polo tournaments, swim meets, fitness competitions and end-of-year pool parties.

Check what's open

Every booking holds its lanes, so the empty water below is what's available.

Booking through Facilitron

Request water on facilitron.com/bbmac92118 or ask the Aquatics Director, Brianna Englehart (brianna.englehart@coronadousd.net), for a quote. Facilitron sets up organization and user accounts, verifies non-profit status if applicable, and collects payment and insurance certificates on Coronado Unified's behalf. Facilitron support: support@facilitron.com, 800-272-2962 ext 1.
