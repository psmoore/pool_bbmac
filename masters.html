Masters Swimming

Two U.S. Masters Swimming clubs train at BBMAC.

Coronado Masters Association

Coached workouts in the 25-yard competition pool. CMA's rental contract with BBMAC requires USMS membership. swimcma.org

Workouts…

Different Strokes Swim Team

San Diego's supportive and welcoming USMS club benefiting the LGBTQIA+ community, open to swimmers 18+ of all levels who can swim one length of a 25-yard pool, with a one-month free trial. DSST practices at locations across greater San Diego, including BBMAC; see dsst.org for current days and times. Membership questions: membership@dsst.org.

DSST's BBMAC practices can go on this schedule too, so swimmers see them next to lap swim.
